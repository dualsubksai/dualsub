## 1. The Service

DualSub for All is a language-learning tool for Chromium-based browsers. It shows dual subtitles on YouTube, translates words and text on web pages and PDFs, saves vocabulary to a notebook stored on your device, and offers flashcards and AI-generated grammar notes.

The free plan is available to everyone at no cost. DualSub Pro is an optional paid subscription that adds:

- unlimited saved words (the free plan keeps up to 100),
- translation of full sentences and paragraphs,
- translation of the example sentence when you save a word,
- AI grammar notes without needing your own API key.
