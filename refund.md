# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

We want DualSub Pro to be worth every cent. If it isn't right for you, you can get your money back.

## 14-day money-back guarantee

- If you are not happy with DualSub Pro, request a refund **within 14 days of your first payment** and we will refund it in full. No questions asked.
- This applies to both the Monthly ($3.99) and Yearly ($24.99) plans.
- After a refund, the subscription is cancelled and the license key is deactivated.

## Renewals

- You can cancel at any time from the customer portal link in your receipt email, and you will not be charged again.
- If a renewal charged you by mistake (for example, you forgot to cancel), contact us within **7 days of the renewal** and we will refund that renewal.
- Outside these windows, payments are not refundable, but Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

## How to request a refund

Email **dualsub.ksai@gmail.com** with the email address you used at checkout (or your order number from the receipt). Refunds are processed by our payment provider, **Lemon Squeezy**, to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, the money usually appears within 5–10 business days.

## Questions

Write to **dualsub.ksai@gmail.com** — we usually reply within 2 business days.
