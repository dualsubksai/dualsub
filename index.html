Turn every unknown word into a tiny lesson

DualSub sits inside the videos, articles and documents you already use, so you learn without leaving what you're doing.

Dual subtitles on YouTube

See the original line and its translation at the same time. Pick any subtitle track and any target language. Hover a word to see its meaning; the video pauses while you look and resumes when you move away.

Hover to translate, on any website

Turn on Reading Mode, hold Alt and hover a word to see its meaning and part of speech. Select a phrase or a paragraph to translate it in place.

A built-in PDF reader

Open online PDFs or files on your computer in DualSub's reader and look up words exactly as you do on the web.

A notebook that remembers the context

Click a word to save it with its meaning, the exact sentence you found it in, a translation of that sentence, and a link back to the moment in the video or the page. Words are grouped by language and by day.

Flashcards, Anki export and AI grammar notes

Review with built-in flashcards (Space to reveal, 1 / 2 to grade) or export to CSV for Anki. AI notes explain the grammar, word forms and collocations, with three fresh example sentences and a memory tip, written in your language.

Private by design

Built for learners, not for advertisers.

Your notebook stays on your device

Saved words, notes and settings are stored locally in your browser. Back them up to a file whenever you like.

Only what you ask to translate

The extension sends only the word or text you look up to the translation service. No browsing history, no profile.

No ads, no trackers

No analytics scripts and no data sales. Read the full privacy policy.

Simple pricing

Everything you need to start is free. Upgrade to Pro when you want unlimited words, full-sentence translation and AI notes with no setup.

Questions

Can't find your answer? Email dualsub.ksai@gmail.com.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout your license key arrives by email within a minute. Open the extension, go to Vocabulary notebook → ★ Pro, paste the key and click Activate.

Which languages are supported?

Any language YouTube offers subtitles for, and all major languages for word lookups. Meanings and AI explanations are written in the language you choose. The interface is available in English and Vietnamese.

Does it work on videos without subtitles?

No. DualSub needs a subtitle track to show and translate. Auto-generated subtitles work fine.

Can I use my own AI key instead of Pro?

Yes. Paste a Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic API key in the notebook settings and AI notes work on the free plan.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from the customer portal link in your receipt email; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Within 14 days of your first payment you can get a full refund. See the refund policy.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned (image-only) PDFs have no text to look up yet. OCR is on the roadmap.
